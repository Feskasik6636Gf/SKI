# bracelet - "In this town, everyone gets exactly 100 words a day." (user-supplied ElevenLabs voice, English)
In this town, everyone gets exactly 100 words a day. A bracelet counts them. Mine is blue. I'm Ben. I'm 9 and I count mine on my fingers. Rich kids buy extra words at the word bank. My friend Leo bought 200. He talks like a radio. At zero, your bracelet buzzes loud and everyone stares. Grandpa says it wasn't always like this. He says before bracelets, he talked to the baker for an hour every day. They complained about the mayor's tower and they laughed a lot. The mayor's sign says, words run out, so save them. But mom talks all day. She calls grandma and sings in the shower. Her bracelet still says 71. Weird thing. My bracelet buzzes when the mayor's light blinks. Not when I talk. I checked three times. Then the word bank put up a sign. Words cost double. Leo's mom sold her ring so Leo could talk at his birthday. The bracelet never counted anything. The mayor just buzzes it when the town gets loud. I had a sandwich wrapped in foil. I wrapped my bracelet tight, like a burrito. Then I walked into the bakery and talked. About my dog, my shoes, grandpa's hour. 300 words. No buzz. Leo saw me. He wrapped his wrist in a gum wrapper. Soon the whole street was yelling about the tower. I forgot to count on my fingers. The mayor's light blinked red. Nothing buzzed. That night, my foil bracelet buzzed anyway. It spelled my name.
